# Brand & Styling Guidelines

[Flipcommons](https://flipcommons.org) is a spinoff of **[The Flip](https://www.theflip.museum/)** pinball museum and adheres to The Flip's brand.

## Colors

The shared brand palette:

| Color | Hex       |
| ----- | --------- |
| Teal  | `#33BEF2` |
| White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Black | `#000000` |

Logos, favicons and marks must ONLY use these.

## Logo

The mark is The Flip's logo — a stylized pinball flipper with the phrase "THE FLIP" superimposed in uppercase — with "commons" in lowercase added either below or to the right.

Two treatments:

- **stacked lockup**: more square/portrait, "commons" underneath
- **long**: more landscape, "commons" to the right

## Favicon & Mobile/Desktop Icons

The favicon and the mobile/desktop app icons are the flipper alone — no words — which is the only element legible at icon sizes.
